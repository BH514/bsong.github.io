Not simply “traditional vs. AI.”

Rules vs. learning describes how a system is built. Deterministic vs. sampled describes how it selects an output. Traditional ML often calculates probabilities, then picks the top label. Generative models can also pick the top next token, or sample instead.

02

A small model. A clear window.

This demo uses hand-authored conditional probabilities, not a trained AI. Each decision selects a word or phrase; real language models use tokens, which may be parts of words. We are illustrating output selection, not training, reasoning, accuracy, or a model’s internal workings. No API calls. No data leaves your browser.
